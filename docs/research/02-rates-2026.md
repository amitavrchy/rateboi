## Normalisation rules for the rate engine
- Weight includes packaging; round **up** to slab; beyond last slab add `extraPerKg` per started kg.
- VAT flag per card; public comparison shows **VAT-inclusive** totals (BD VAT on services — rate configurable, default 15%, verify).
- COD base differs (Steadfast: % of cash collected after delivery charge; Pathao: % of product price) → `codBase` enum.
- Effective cost to merchant = delivery + COD charge + P(return) × return charge (+ re-delivery) — this is the "real cost" shown.
- Other sources for cross-checking: https://sellspilot.com/delivery-charge-calculator (Oct 7 2026)
